## Purpose

Questions about displayed liquidity, such as when it appears, how long it persists, where it moves and what happens to price afterwards, are central to debates on market transparency and market integrity. They are also difficult to examine, because order-book evidence is transient and often unrecorded.

FZ HMI is built to record, reconstruct and replay public order-book data so that liquidity behaviour can be studied reproducibly. It is a read-only research tool. It does not execute trades, give investment advice or reach legal conclusions.

## Research focus

- Order-book microstructure and liquidity migration
- Spot versus perpetual futures behaviour
- Candidate liquidity sweeps and post-sweep acceptance or rejection
- Technological asymmetry and market transparency
- Evidentiary limits of order-book data for market surveillance

Liquidity Destination Theory and the Hierarchy of Liquidity Magnets are treated as hypotheses that require empirical testing, not as established findings.

## Research design principles

1. **Real data or clearly labelled demonstration data.** Live, historical and simulated data are kept separate and labelled.
2. **No fabricated results.** The platform does not invent prices, order-book quantities, statistics or findings.
3. **Reproducibility.** Analyses record their parameters, data source and software version, with CSV and JSON export.
4. **Cautious language.** Patterns are described as "unusual" or "potential indicators", never as proven manipulation.
5. **Alternative explanations and limitations** are recorded alongside every research event.
6. **Read-only market-data access.** No trading execution.

## Limitations and responsible use

The platform analyses displayed liquidity in public market data only. It cannot show hidden orders, account identity or intent, and liquidity disappearing at one price level and appearing at another does not show that the same order or participant is involved. Spot and perpetual divergence is not, by itself, evidence of manipulation. Nothing in this application is proof of a market-abuse offence, nor legal, financial or investment advice. Features marked "In progress" may be incomplete.
